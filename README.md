# Moscow Metro Depths

A 3D map of the Moscow and Paris metros (switch cities in the side panel, or open `?city=paris`): the city map lies on a translucent ground plane and every line runs underneath it at its real depth (exaggerated, since 84 m is tiny next to a 50 km city).

**Live:** https://sergey-chuvayev.github.io/moscow-metro-3d/

## Run

No build step. Serve the folder over HTTP:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8765
# open http://localhost:8765
```

## What you can do

- Orbit, pan and zoom. Presets: Overview, Cross-section (side view), Top, From below.
- Hover a line or station for details. Click a line to isolate it and open its **depth profile** (distance vs depth). Click a station for its depth card and transfers.
- Depth exaggeration slider (1x-100x), ground opacity, color by depth, shafts to the surface, transfers.
- Hide or show lines, jump to the deepest stations, switch between English and Russian.
- **Timeline**: press play to watch the network grow from 1935 to today, with captions for key moments. Drag the slider to stop at any year.
- Station cards show a photo from Wikimedia Commons (with author and licence) and the opening date.

## Data

`data/moscow.json` is generated by `scripts/build_moscow.py`:

- Stations, coordinates, order, colours: hh.ru metro API
- Depths: Wikidata (P4511), filled in from Russian Wikipedia infoboxes. 17 stations without a published depth use estimates, marked `"source": "estimate"` and flagged in the UI
- Opening dates: Wikidata (P1619, per-line P580 where a station serves several lines), with manual fixes in `OPEN_OVERRIDES`
- MCC is surface rail (0 m). Vorobyovy Gory sits on a bridge (+8 m). MCD suburban rail is left out

`data/paris.json` is generated by `scripts/build_paris.py`:

- Stations, coordinates, names and line order: Wikidata (P197 "adjacent station" per line gives branches and loops)
- Position (underground / elevated / ground) and opening dates: French Wikipedia infoboxes
- Depths: Paris publishes few. 27 platforms use figures quoted in French Wikipedia articles (Abbesses 36 m, Villejuif - Gustave Roussy 36.7 m, …). The rest are typical values (7 m for classic lines, 22 m for line 14, about 7 m above the street on viaducts), marked as estimates
- Lines 15-18 (Grand Paris Express) are not open yet and are left out; ghost stations are excluded

Photos for both cities come from Wikidata (P18), resolved to Wikimedia Commons thumbnails with author and licence.
Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build_moscow.py` and `python3 scripts/build_paris.py` (responses are cached in `scripts/.cache/`).

## Map tiles

On localhost it uses Stadia Maps "Alidade Smooth Dark" (keyless on localhost only). On any other host it falls back to Esri World Dark Gray. To keep Stadia after deploying, register the domain with Stadia or add an API key in `src/main.js`.

Stack: Three.js 0.169 (vendored in `vendor/three`, so nothing loads from a JS CDN), plain ES modules, no dependencies to install. The UI opens in Russian when the browser language is Russian.
